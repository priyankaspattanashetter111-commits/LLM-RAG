I have a dream that one day this nation will rise up and live out the true meaning of its creed: that all people are created equal.

I have a dream that one day on the hills of Georgia, the sons of former slaves and the sons of former slave owners will be able to sit down together at the table of brotherhood.

I have a dream that my four little children will one day live in a nation where they will not be judged by the color of their skin but by the content of their character.

I have a dream today that freedom and equality will become a reality for everyone.
